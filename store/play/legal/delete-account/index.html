Delete your Akira Flow account

You can request that we delete your Akira Flow account and the business data stored with it (sales, customers, products, expenses, and related records).

How to request deletion

Email susanmwangi968@gmail.com from the same address you use to sign in.

Include:

  • The subject line: Akira Flow account deletion
  • Your account email
  • Your business name

What we delete

  • Your user account (name, email, Google sign-in link)
  • Business profile and settings
  • Products, inventory, sales, expenses, orders, and customer records stored in the cloud

Timing

We will delete the account and associated data within 30 days, unless we must keep limited records for a legal reason (for example an M-Pesa payment dispute).

Data stored only on your phone can be removed by uninstalling the app.
