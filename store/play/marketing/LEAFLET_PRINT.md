## Print tips

1. Open the PNG in Google Docs / Canva / LibreOffice → set page to **A5**.  
2. Print on **matt 150–200 gsm** flyer paper if possible (ordinary A4 is fine for pilots).  
3. For bulk: export PDF from Canva after placing the PNG full-bleed.  
4. Optional: add your **WhatsApp business number** and a handwritten QR to the Play listing on the back.
