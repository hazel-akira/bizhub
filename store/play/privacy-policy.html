Akira Flow Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

Akira Flow (“the app”) is a point-of-sale and business management app for small shops in Kenya. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how you can control it.

Who we are

The app is published under package name app.akirabizhub.pos. Contact: susanmwangi968@gmail.com.

Data we collect

  • Account: name, email, password (hashed on our servers) or Google account identifiers if you sign in with Google.
  • Business profile: business name, business category, and settings you enter.
  • Business records: products, inventory, sales, expenses, orders, unpaid balances, and profit figures you record.
  • Customers: names and phone numbers you add. If you tap “From contacts”, the app reads your device contacts so you can pick a customer. Contacts are not uploaded unless you save that person as a customer.
  • Payments: M-Pesa phone numbers, amounts, and receipt numbers when you send a Lipa Na M-Pesa STK request.
  • Photos: product photos you choose from the camera or gallery.
  • Device: a notification permission so the app can remind you to record sales. We do not collect precise location.

How we use data

To create your account, sync your shop data across devices, process M-Pesa collections for your own till or paybill, show reports, and send optional sales reminders.

Who we share data with

  • Google — if you use Google Sign-In, so we can verify your identity.
  • Safaricom (Daraja / M-Pesa) — phone number and amount, so a payment prompt can be sent to your customer.
  • We do not sell your data. Hosting providers that store our database may process it only to run the service.

Offline use

Some shop data can also be stored on your device. Cloud sync happens when you are signed in and connected to our API over HTTPS.

Retention and deletion

We keep your account and business records while the account is active. Email susanmwangi968@gmail.com to request deletion of your account and associated business data. We will delete it within 30 days unless we must keep records for legal reasons (for example payment disputes).

Children

Akira Flow is for shop owners and staff. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Security

Traffic to our API uses HTTPS. Passwords are stored hashed. M-Pesa till/paybill credentials you enter in Settings are stored on the server, not inside the Play Store listing.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
